What is the MP2000?

The MP2000 is an autonomous pallet truck (pallet AMR) for the horizontal transport of pallets weighing up to 2,000 kg in docks, warehouses, line-side areas and workshops. It navigates with 3D SLAM (3D LiDAR and vision), detects pallet position and location occupancy, and supports automatic or manual driving. It covers floor storage and the first shelving level.

  • No facility changes needed

    Locates itself with 3D LiDAR and vision, without reflectors or floor markings; mapping and commissioning take minutes.

  • Pallet tolerance

    Enters pallets offset by up to 15 cm or rotated up to 15°, whether three-runner or perimeter-base, in about 20 s.

  • Autonomous locations and fleet

    Detects which locations are occupied, chooses destination and route, and coordinates multiple units without a network or server.

  • 360° perception

    3D and 2D LiDAR, cameras, contour projector and LiDAR at the fork tips: stops for moving objects and goes around fixed obstacles.

Applications of the MP2000.

Scenarios where the MP2000 fits in.

  • Receiving and dispatching pallets at docks
  • Floor storage and first shelving level
  • Line supply from staging areas
  • Moving finished product to warehouse and dispatch
  • Distribution centers and urgent load transfer
  • Replenishment for picking and consolidation in e-commerce
