What is the OmniPicker?

OmniPicker is a two-finger robotic gripper for robotic arms and humanoid robots. It combines 140 N of grip force, 120 mm of stroke, and tactile sensing at the fingertips with 0.1 N resolution. It is used in sorting and logistics, in data capture to train embodied AI systems, and in continuous production operations.

  • Industrial reliability

    The 140 N grip force is validated over one million cycles at full load.

  • Adaptive grip with touch sensing

    Tactile sensors on the gripping surface, with 0.1 N resolution, for handling standard parts, irregular shapes, and delicate objects with force control.

  • Built for data collection

    Compatible with Dex-UMI and with an optional depth or fisheye camera, for capturing manipulation data and training models.

  • Open integration

    CAN FD or RS485 communication, ROS 2, and an SDK in Python and C++.

Applications of the OmniPicker.

Scenarios where the OmniPicker fits in.

  • Pick-and-place in industrial sorting
  • Parcel handling
  • Warehouse automation
  • Large-scale demonstration capture for embodied AI
  • Force-based manipulation data collection
  • Continuous operations in retail and e-commerce
