What is the RHG2?

The RHG2 is an industrial humanoid with a mobile base, telescopic torso and two manipulator arms. Unlike bipedal humanoids, it uses a wheeled platform to gain stability, autonomy and industrial capacity, and is designed to carry out complete tasks with minimal supervision in production, logistics and inspection.

  • Telescopic torso

    Adjustable height between 1,225 and 1,795 mm to work at different station heights without reconfiguring the cell.

  • Precision manipulation

    7-DOF arms with joint torque sensors and force control, with sub-millimetre precision operations announced by the manufacturer.

  • Autonomous mobile platform

    Ackermann steering, lateral movement, in-place rotation, 360° perception and automatic charging station.

  • High-performance computing

    NVIDIA Jetson Thor T5000 with 2,070 TFLOPS announced at FP4 precision, for perception and skill learning.
