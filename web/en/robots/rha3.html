What is the RHA3?

The RHA3 is an adult-sized bipedal humanoid designed for commercial shows, interactive entertainment, reception, presentations and exhibitions. With 55 kg and 12 kW of peak power, it performs dynamic stage movements, and its swappable dual battery allows it to work for extended periods. It includes UWB positioning as standard to coordinate groups of more than 100 robots.

  • Power in 55 kg

    12 kW of peak power and 150 A of maximum current, with a power-to-weight ratio of 0.218 kW/kg. Structure in magnesium alloy, titanium and flexible TPU.

  • Full-day battery life

    Redundant dual battery of 1,152 Wh with up to 10 hours of use and hot-swapping in about 10 seconds, for continuous 24-hour operation.

  • Fleet coordination

    Standard UWB positioning with accuracy under ±10 cm to coordinate more than 100 units without modification.

  • Multimodal interaction

    Binocular vision, touch sensors on the shoulders, 360° voice pickup with multiple microphone arrays, and a front display.
