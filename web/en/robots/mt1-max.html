What is the MT1 Max?

The MT1 Max builds on the MT1 and adapts it to semi-outdoor environments with traffic, such as car parks, residential courtyards or squares. It adds 3D lidar, a dual processor, IP54 protection and an elevated warning light, and can climb slopes and speed bumps. It sweeps everything from cigarette butts and paper to large bottles with a 70 cm width.

  • 3D perception

    3D lidar and sensor fusion for mapping with high ceilings, glass canopies, low light, dust or fog.

  • Operates alongside vehicles

    It stops, waits or moves aside to give way, and reacts to sudden movements from people and animals.

  • AI-driven adaptive cleaning

    With a dual processor, it adjusts brush force according to the debris and positions itself to keep it between the side brushes.

  • Built for semi-outdoor use

    IP54 sealing, water-droplet detection, operation between −10 and 45 °C, and ability to pass 5 cm speed bumps.
