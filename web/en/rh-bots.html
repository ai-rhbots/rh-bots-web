Who we are

The team behind RH·BOTS.

Our story.

RH·BOTS was created to bring humanoid robotics to companies that need real results, not demonstrations. As the official distributor of AGIBOT and PUDU in Spain and Portugal, we select the right robot for each operation and support our clients through the whole process: prior advice, installation, team training and ongoing maintenance.

We work from Picassent (Valencia), with clients in sectors ranging from industrial cleaning to customer service and factory automation.

Our mission

Making humanoid robotics accessible, reliable and profitable.

Every project starts with listening. We analyse needs, identify opportunities and recommend the robot model that best fits the business objective.

Our team combines technical knowledge, product vision and hands-on integration so that the rollout is clear and measurable.

  • Prior advice and selection of the right robot.
  • Installation, commissioning and training.
  • Support to keep the robot delivering value.
